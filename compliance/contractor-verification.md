# Contractor Verification – ServiPro

This document explains how contractors are verified before gaining access to the ServiPro platform.

---

## 1. Company Registration Check

All contractors must be UK registered limited companies.

### Validation Process

- Company registration number required
- Cross-check with UK Companies House
- Verify active trading status
- Store registration details in contractor profile

---

## 2. Insurance Verification

Contractors must hold valid insurance appropriate to their services.

### Required Documents

- Public Liability Insurance
- Employer’s Liability Insurance (if applicable)
- Professional Indemnity (where relevant)

### Platform Handling

- Mandatory document upload during onboarding
- Expiry tracking system
- Risk flag if expired or missing

---

## 3. Identity & Director Validation

### Checks

- Director name verification
- Email + phone validation
- Matching with company records

---

## 4. Financial & Risk Screening

### Checks

- Company health status monitoring
- Insolvency and overdue accounts detection
- Risk level scoring (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH)

---

## 5. Approval Workflow

1. Contractor submits registration
2. System validates company data
3. Admin reviews profile
4. Status updated: APPROVED / REJECTED / PENDING

---

## Summary

This verification system ensures only legitimate, insured, and compliant UK contractors can operate on ServiPro.
