# UK Property Regulations – ServiPro Context

This document outlines key UK regulatory considerations that influence the ServiPro platform design and workflows.

---

## 1. Service Charge Legislation

Under UK law, service charges must be:

- Reasonable in amount
- Properly demanded
- Supported by clear documentation

### Platform Alignment

ServiPro ensures:

- Full audit trail of all procurement decisions
- Transparent tender comparison history
- Stored contracts and invoices for compliance review
- Structured approval workflows for managing agents

---

## 2. Procurement Transparency Requirements

Managing agents are expected to demonstrate fair and transparent procurement processes.

### Platform Alignment

- Multiple contractor bidding system
- Standardized tender templates
- Comparable bid structure
- Time-stamped audit logs for all actions
- No hidden award decisions

---

## 3. Contractor Compliance Expectations

UK property managers are required to ensure contractors are:

- Properly insured
- Legally registered (UK limited companies preferred)
- Competent for assigned works

### Platform Alignment

- Contractor verification during onboarding
- Company registration validation
- Insurance and compliance document uploads
- Company health check monitoring

---

## 4. Health & Safety Regulations

All contractors working in residential and commercial buildings must comply with UK health & safety laws.

### Platform Alignment

- Mandatory compliance document uploads per bid
- Category-specific compliance requirements
- Risk flags for overdue certifications
- Audit logs for inspections and approvals

---

## 5. Data Protection (UK GDPR)

Personal and business data must be handled securely and lawfully.

### Platform Alignment

- Role-based access control
- Secure file storage (AWS S3)
- Encrypted sensitive data handling
- User consent tracking where required

---

## 6. Financial Transparency

Service charge funds must be clearly accounted for.

### Platform Alignment

- Contract value tracking
- Commission transparency
- Invoice history per contract
- Full lifecycle financial records

---

## Summary

ServiPro is designed to align with UK property management regulations by enforcing transparency, compliance, and structured procurement workflows across all platform activities.
