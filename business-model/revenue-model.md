# Revenue Model – ServiPro

## 🧠 Overview

ServiPro operates a hybrid B2B SaaS + marketplace commission model designed specifically for UK property procurement workflows.

The platform generates revenue exclusively from contractors, ensuring managing agents remain free users to maximise adoption.

---

## 💼 1. Core Revenue Structure

ServiPro has two primary revenue streams:

### A) Subscription Revenue (SaaS Layer)

- Monthly fixed fee paid by contractors
- Grants access to:
  - Tender opportunities
  - Bidding system
  - Compliance tools
  - Platform visibility

### B) Commission Revenue (Marketplace Layer)

- Percentage-based fee on awarded contracts
- Applied to:
  - New contracts
  - Renewals sourced via platform

---

## 📊 2. Subscription Model

### Structure:

- Fixed monthly pricing
- Single professional tier (initial phase)
- Optional future tier expansion (not yet active)

### Purpose:

- Stable recurring revenue
- Filters serious contractors only
- Reduces low-quality participation

---

## 📈 3. Commission Model

### Mechanism:

- Commission charged only on successful outcomes
- Triggered when:
  - Tender is awarded
  - Contract is created

### Formula:

**Commission = Annual Contract Value × Commission Rate**

### Key Principle:

No success → No commission.

---

## 🔁 4. Renewal Monetisation

ServiPro continues to earn on:

- Contract renewals processed through platform
- Re-awarded tenders for existing relationships

This ensures:

- Long-term revenue compounding
- Reduced churn sensitivity

---

## 🧾 5. Payment Responsibility Model

### Contractors:

- Pay subscriptions
- Pay commission invoices

### Managing Agents:

- Use platform free of charge

### Key Insight:

This ensures zero friction for demand-side adoption.

---

## 🛡️ 6. Anti-Bypass Revenue Protection

To prevent disintermediation:

- Non-circumvention agreements
- Contract traceability enforcement
- Renewal tracking system
- Audit-linked procurement history

---

## 📉 7. Risk Controls

Revenue risks mitigated through:

- Mandatory contractor verification (UK LTD only)
- Subscription gating before bidding
- Commission enforcement at contract level
- Audit-based dispute resolution system

---

## 📊 8. Scalability Strategy

Revenue scales through:

- More contractors joining subscription base
- Higher contract volumes via tenders
- Expansion into additional UK regions
- Increased renewal cycles over time

---

## 🧠 9. Business Positioning

ServiPro is not a generic marketplace.

It is positioned as:

> “A regulated procurement infrastructure layer for UK property management.”

This creates:

- High switching costs
- Long-term platform dependency
- Enterprise-level adoption potential

---

## ⚙️ Key Design Principle

> “Revenue is only generated when real-world procurement value is successfully delivered.”
>
