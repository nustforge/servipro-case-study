# Pricing Strategy – ServiPro

## 🧠 Overview

ServiPro’s pricing strategy is designed to balance:

- High-quality contractor onboarding
- Predictable recurring SaaS revenue
- Strong incentive alignment with platform success
- Long-term enterprise scalability

The model is intentionally simple at entry level to maximise adoption while preserving strong monetisation leverage on successful outcomes.

---

## 💼 1. Pricing Philosophy

ServiPro follows a **performance-aligned pricing model**:

- Fixed cost for access (subscription)
- Variable cost based on success (commission)

This ensures:

> Contractors only pay significantly more when they earn through the platform.

---

## 📊 2. Contractor Subscription Pricing

### Model:

- Single-tier professional subscription (initial phase)

### Includes:

- Access to all tenders
- Bid submission system
- Compliance dashboard
- Document storage
- Platform visibility

### Strategic Purpose:

- Filters serious contractors
- Ensures minimum revenue baseline
- Reduces spam / low-quality participation

---

## 💰 3. Commission Pricing

### Structure:

- Percentage-based fee per awarded contract
- Applied on:
  - New contracts
  - Renewals sourced via ServiPro

### Formula:

**Commission = Annual Contract Value × Commission Rate**

### Key Principle:

Higher-value contracts generate higher platform revenue.

---

## 🔁 4. Renewal Pricing Logic

Renewals are treated as **new monetisation events** if:

- Contract was initiated via ServiPro
- Relationship is tracked within platform lifecycle

This ensures:

- Long-term revenue compounding
- No reliance on one-time transaction value

---

## 📉 5. Why Not Free Access?

Free contractor access was rejected because:

- It leads to low-quality bidding
- Reduces platform trust
- Increases operational noise
- Weakens compliance structure

Instead:

> Subscription ensures commitment and professionalism.

---

## 🛡️ 6. Pricing Protection Strategy

To protect revenue integrity:

- Contracts are linked to platform origin
- Commission is legally enforced via onboarding terms
- Non-circumvention clauses apply
- Audit logs support dispute resolution

---

## 📊 7. Unit Economics Logic

ServiPro scales revenue through:

### 1. Contractor Growth

More subscriptions = predictable baseline revenue

### 2. Contract Volume Growth

More tenders awarded = higher commission revenue

### 3. Contract Value Growth

Higher-value property contracts = exponential commission scaling

---

## 🧠 8. Strategic Positioning

ServiPro is priced as:

> “A professional procurement infrastructure system, not a marketplace listing tool.”

This supports:

- Higher willingness to pay
- Enterprise adoption
- Long-term contractual dependency

---

## ⚙️ Key Design Principle

> “Pricing must align directly with real-world value creation, not platform usage.”
